## Short description (132 characters max)

- English: Make WhatsApp stickers that play sound. Turn any video, GIF or picture plus a sound into a sound sticker, right in WhatsApp Web.
- Arabic: اصنع ملصقات واتساب بصوت. حوّل أي فيديو أو GIF أو صورة مع صوت إلى ملصق صوتي داخل واتساب ويب مباشرة.

## Detailed description (English)

Make WhatsApp stickers that play sound, without leaving WhatsApp Web.

• Any picture, any sound: a video with its own sound, a GIF with a song, a photo with a voice note.
• Trim with a waveform and a live preview. 10 seconds or less works best.
• Always under WhatsApp's 1 MB limit: size, frame rate and quality are picked for you.
• Send to the open chat, or to your own chat and forward it anywhere.
• Arabic and English, light and dark.
• Private: your files are processed in your browser and never uploaded anywhere except to WhatsApp when you send.

Sound stickers play for friends whose WhatsApp supports them; others see the moving sticker.

Free and open source: https://github.com/haythamasalama/sound-sticker-maker

## Privacy practices

- Single purpose: make and send sound stickers in WhatsApp Web.
- `storage`: remembers language, pack name, author and destination.
- Host permission `web.whatsapp.com`: shows the panel and sends stickers there.
- Remote code: none.
- Data collected: none. Certify that data is not sold, not used for unrelated purposes and not used for credit.
